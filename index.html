Still need help?
What is HKD Dewas QR Code?

HKD Dewas QR Code builds business-focused software including customized ERP solutions, web platforms, digital products and technology solutions designed around real workflows.

What does the HKD Dewas QR Code platform do?

It creates a QR code that opens a mobile-friendly business profile with contact information, website, address and a review link, so customers can find important details in one scan.

Can I update my QR profile details later?

Yes, profile details can be updated whenever the profile management feature is available for your account. Availability depends on the features currently enabled.

Can I download the QR code?

The QR platform is designed to create a scannable link to a mobile-friendly business profile. Contact HKD Dewas QR Code to discuss availability.

Can I share my QR profile with anyone?

Yes, you can share your QR code digitally or display it on printed materials, making it easier for people to access your profile and contact information.
